**HitLam — app overview**

HitLam is a marketplace for discovering, showcasing, buying, and selling individual internet products. A founder can offer one product while keeping their other products and the rest of their business.

The app welcomes both revenue-generating products and products that have not earned money yet. Founders can also showcase a product without making it available for sale.

**Who it serves**

Sellers are founders who want visibility, buyer interest, or an opportunity to sell a particular product. Buyers are people or companies looking for products they could acquire and develop further.

Users register as either sellers or buyers.

**How sellers use it**

Sellers create a free listing containing the product’s name, website, description, screenshots, target audience, pricing model, asking price, running costs, and assets included in a potential sale.

They can optionally connect Stripe to attach revenue information associated with that product, including when they operate several products under the same Stripe account. Listings display available financial evidence alongside information supplied by the seller.

Sellers can edit their listings, change sale availability, respond to buyer inquiries, and purchase additional visibility.

**How buyers use it**

Buyers browse or search the catalog and open individual product pages to examine the product, financial information, costs, asking price, and included assets.

Before contacting sellers, buyers complete a profile describing themselves, their budget, and whether they are purchasing personally or through a company.

A buyer submits an offer and introductory message to open a private deal room. Both parties can discuss the acquisition and exchange supporting files there. Creating a profile or submitting an offer does not take payment.

After the seller accepts the deal, either party can initiate the Escrow.com process. Both must first provide an escrow contact email.

**How discovery works**

The main board displays up to 330 products across two visual columns belonging to one overall ranking.

Paid listings appear first, ordered by their bids. Free listings follow in their original listing order. The “All MRR” view provides access to the wider searchable catalog, including products beyond the main board.

Paid promotion is labeled. Ranking reflects purchased visibility and listing order; buyers still need to evaluate each product themselves.

**How HitLam makes money**

Listing a product is free. The implemented revenue model has three parts:

- **Rank boosts:** Bids start at $2 where there is no existing paid target. Beating a paid position requires at least $0.50 above its current bid. Paid bids have no scheduled expiry, although larger bids can move them down.
- **Carousel promotion:** Sellers can purchase one of 12 moving showcase spots for $10, lasting 24 hours after confirmed payment.
- **Acquisition fees:** The configured buyer-paid HitLam fee is 8% for purchases below $10,000, 3.5% from $10,000 to below $50,000, and 1.7% from $50,000 upward. The applicable rate applies to the whole purchase amount. Escrow.com’s own fees are separate and configured to be shared equally by buyer and seller.

Stripe handles promotional payments. Escrow.com is the intended provider for acquisition funding, inspection, and payment to the seller. HitLam does not itself hold acquisition funds.

**Current status**

The app brings product discovery, financial context, paid visibility, private negotiation, and an escrow handoff into one experience. Revenue evidence does not establish ownership, transferability, or future performance.

These flows are present in the implementation. Seller screens have been inspected live, but successful payment completion and a full acquisition through escrow have not been verified end to end.
