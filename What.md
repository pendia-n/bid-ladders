# BidLadders: What It Provides

## English

### Product definition

BidLadders is a product-level marketplace and discovery board for buying and selling small internet businesses, including products with no revenue. It combines verified recurring-revenue evidence with a transparent paid-ranking ladder.

It is not a marketplace for selling an entire Stripe account or automatically selling the seller's whole LLC. Each listing represents the specific product and the assets described in its deal terms. A company may list one product while retaining its other products and legal entity.

### Seller experience

Sellers can:

- create one listing for one product or startup;
- enter the product URL, product name, asking price, operating costs, and assets included;
- verify MRR for that product from a multi-product Stripe account;
- list a $0-MRR product by completing the required detailed description;
- appear for free in chronological order when space is available;
- pay to bid for a higher, visibly sponsored rank;
- receive buyer inquiries and share due-diligence material;
- complete a sale using a third-party escrow process.

### Buyer experience

Buyers can:

- scan 330 homepage listings without pagination;
- understand the exact ordering and which positions are paid;
- see product-level MRR verification status and timestamp;
- search the complete catalog on `/mrr`, including listings outside the homepage;
- compare asking price, MRR, costs, category, age, and included assets;
- contact sellers and proceed into a recorded due-diligence workflow.

### Homepage behavior

The homepage displays exactly 330 available positions as two side-by-side ladders of 165. They are two visual columns of one global order: odd-numbered ranks appear on the left and even-numbered ranks on the right. There is no homepage pagination.

Paid bids rank above free listings. Free listings fill the remaining capacity by earliest listing time. Once 330 positions are occupied, later free listings remain searchable on `/mrr` but do not appear on `/` unless their rank improves or space opens.

### Fees

- Creating a listing: free.
- Recording or displaying verified MRR: free.
- First paid boost when no paid ranks exist: $2.
- Later paid boost: at least $0.50 above the selected paid rank.
- Completed sale below $10,000: 8% success fee.
- Completed sale from $10,000 to below $50,000: 3.5% success fee.
- Completed sale at $50,000 or more: 1.7% success fee.

Bid payments and success fees are separate. Their refund, expiration, displacement, closing, and collection rules must be shown before payment.

### What stands apart from TrustMRR

BidLadders adds a discovery and ranking market rather than treating revenue verification as the entire product. It is designed for one Stripe account containing many products: a seller discovers matches by name, confirms exact Product and Price IDs, and verifies only the listed product. It also gives $0-MRR products a legitimate route to list when they disclose enough qualitative information.

BidLadders should not claim that its numbers are identical to TrustMRR's methodology unless that has been independently demonstrated. Its own MRR definition and verification freshness must be public.

### What stands apart from outbid.lol

BidLadders applies transparent bidding to acquisition listings rather than to a generic link board. Every position represents a structured, searchable product listing with ownership, financial, cost, and sale information. Only 330 positions appear on the homepage, arranged as two 165-position visual ladders without pagination, while the complete market remains accessible on `/mrr`.

The bid is for temporary visibility, not evidence that the product is better, safer, or more profitable. Sponsored placement must always be labeled.

### What BidLadders does not promise

- It does not guarantee that a listing will sell.
- It does not guarantee future revenue or investment returns.
- Stripe verification does not prove code ownership, clean intellectual property, transferable accounts, or truthful seller claims outside Stripe.
- Ranking is not an endorsement.
- BidLadders is not the escrow holder unless a properly licensed operating model is established later.
- The platform does not sell a legal entity unless a listing and signed agreement explicitly define an entity sale.

## 中文

### 产品定义

BidLadders 是一个以具体产品为单位的小型互联网业务交易与曝光平台，并允许尚未有收入的产品上架。它把经验证的经常性收入证据与透明的付费竞价排名结合起来。

它不是用来出售整个 Stripe 账户，也不会默认出售卖家的整家 LLC。每个 listing 只代表交易条款中明确描述的特定产品与资产。一家公司可以出售其中一个产品，同时保留其他产品及原有法律实体。

### 卖家获得什么

卖家可以：

- 为一个产品或 startup 建立一个 listing；
- 填写产品网址、名称、叫价、营运成本及交易所含资产；
- 从包含多个产品的 Stripe 账户中，只验证该 listing 对应产品的 MRR；
- 通过完整填写必要说明，上架 $0 MRR 产品；
- 在首页仍有位置时，免费按上架时间获得排名；
- 付费竞价取得更高且明确标示为赞助的位置；
- 接收买家询问并分享尽职调查资料；
- 通过第三方 escrow 流程完成交易。

### 买家获得什么

买家可以：

- 不经分页直接浏览首页 330 个 listing；
- 清楚理解排序方法及哪些位置属于付费排名；
- 查看产品级 MRR 验证状态及时间；
- 在 `/mrr` 搜寻完整目录，包括未进入首页的 listing；
- 比较叫价、MRR、成本、类别、营运年期及所含资产；
- 联络卖家，并进入有记录的尽职调查流程。

### 首页行为

首页提供 330 个位置，以左右两个各 165 位的阶梯显示。它们是同一个全局排名的两栏视觉呈现：单数排名在左，双数排名在右。首页没有分页。

付费出价排在免费 listing 之前，免费 listing 按最早上架时间填补余下位置。330 个位置满额后，之后的免费 listing 仍可在 `/mrr` 被搜寻，但不会显示在 `/`，除非提升排名或首页出现空位。

### 费用

- 建立 listing：免费。
- 记录或显示验证 MRR：免费。
- 没有付费排名时的首次提升：$2。
- 之后的付费提升：至少比所选择的付费位置高 $0.50。
- 成交价低于 $10,000：8% 成功交易费。
- 成交价为 $10,000 至低于 $50,000：3.5% 成功交易费。
- 成交价为 $50,000 或以上：1.7% 成功交易费。

竞价费用与成功交易费是两种不同费用。付款前必须显示退款、有效期、被超越、成交及收费规则。

### 与 TrustMRR 的区别

BidLadders 不把收入验证当成全部产品，而是额外建立曝光与竞价市场。它专门处理一个 Stripe 账户包含多个产品的现实情况：卖家先用名称寻找匹配项，再确认准确 Product ID 与 Price ID，只验证被上架的产品。它也允许 $0 MRR 产品通过充分披露非财务资料获得合理的上架机会。

除非经过独立证明，否则 BidLadders 不应声称计算结果与 TrustMRR 完全一致。BidLadders 必须公开自己的 MRR 定义与验证资料新鲜度。

### 与 outbid.lol 的区别

BidLadders 把透明竞价应用于收购 listing，而不是一般链接榜。每个位置都是结构化、可搜寻的产品 listing，并包含所有权、财务、成本及出售资料。首页只显示 330 个位置，以两个各 165 位的视觉阶梯呈现且不分页；完整市场则保留在 `/mrr`。

出价只购买暂时曝光，不代表产品更好、更安全或更赚钱。赞助位置必须始终明确标示。

### BidLadders 不承诺什么

- 不保证 listing 一定成交。
- 不保证未来收入或投资回报。
- Stripe 验证不能证明代码所有权、知识产权没有争议、账户可转让，或 Stripe 以外的卖家声明真实。
- 排名不代表平台背书。
- 除非未来建立符合牌照要求的营运模式，否则 BidLadders 不是 escrow 持款方。
- 除非 listing 与签署协议明确规定出售法律实体，否则平台交易不包含整家公司。
