# BidLadders

## English

### What the app is

BidLadders is a product-level marketplace and discovery board for internet products. A founder can publish one product without implying that the founder's entire company, LLC, Stripe account, or other products are being sold. A published product may be offered for sale or listed only for discovery.

The homepage presents one global order across two visual ladders, with up to 330 products and no pagination. Free listings earn visibility by arriving early. Sellers can pay for a clearly labeled rank or a 24-hour carousel placement. Buyers can inspect product context, review available revenue signals, and open a private deal room when a product is for sale.

### Why it was created

Small and medium software products are often difficult to discover or sell before they have strong revenue. Traditional acquisition marketplaces tend to favor established businesses, while revenue-verification services provide evidence without necessarily creating attention. Generic paid-ranking boards create attention without enough product context for an acquisition decision.

BidLadders was created to connect those missing pieces: product-level evidence, understandable discovery, direct buyer-seller conversations, and a practical path from listing to third-party escrow.

### The user problem it solves

For sellers, BidLadders solves four related problems:

- A founder can list one product from a Stripe account that contains several products.
- A product can be published even when it has $0 MRR or is not currently for sale.
- Product information, revenue context, asking price, operating cost, assets, and buyer conversations stay attached to the same listing.
- Visibility is understandable: free order, paid rank, and sponsored placement are visibly distinct.

For buyers, BidLadders reduces the work of finding and evaluating independent products. Buyers can compare structured listings, distinguish verified signals from seller-provided claims, see whether a product is actually available for sale, and keep negotiations connected to the exact product under review.

### How it reduces stress

BidLadders reduces marketplace uncertainty through assimilation, assurance, and practical next steps.

**Assimilation:** Each listing gathers the essential product context in one place: product identity, audience, problem solved, technology, pricing model, revenue signals, operating cost, asking price, included assets, seller identity, and current sale status. Buyers do not have to assemble the basic story from disconnected messages and screenshots.

**Assurance:** The interface separates what is known from what is merely claimed. Stripe-derived product-family signals are labeled separately from seller-supplied context. Sponsored rank is visible and is not presented as an endorsement. A listing represents a product, not automatically an LLC sale. BidLadders does not hold acquisition funds; an accepted transaction proceeds through a third-party escrow provider.

**Practical next steps:** Sellers can publish, choose whether the product is for sale, verify relevant Stripe products and prices, improve visibility, and respond to buyer inquiries. Buyers can inspect the listing, open a private deal room, negotiate the asset transfer, and continue to escrow only after the seller accepts the deal.

### Why it is unique

BidLadders combines several mechanisms that are usually separated:

- product-level listing and revenue attribution for founders operating multiple products under one Stripe account;
- a legitimate path for $0-MRR products and products listed for discovery rather than immediate sale;
- one transparent 330-position market rendered as two 165-position ladders;
- free chronological discovery alongside clearly labeled paid ranking and 24-hour carousel placement;
- private product-specific deal rooms followed by a licensed third-party escrow handoff;
- a searchable full catalog outside the homepage's scarce ranking inventory.

The result is neither only an MRR-verification page nor only a paid link board. It is a structured product-acquisition market where evidence, visibility, negotiation, and transaction readiness remain connected without pretending that ranking proves quality or that BidLadders itself is the escrow holder.

## 中文

### 这个应用是什么

BidLadders 是一个以具体产品为单位的互联网产品交易与发现平台。创办人可以独立发布一个产品，而不代表其整家公司、LLC、Stripe 账户或其他产品也在出售。已发布的产品可以设为正在出售，也可以只用于展示与被发现。

首页以左右两个视觉阶梯呈现同一个全局排名，最多显示 330 个产品且不设分页。免费 listing 按上架先后取得曝光；卖家也可以购买明确标示的排名或 24 小时轮播展示位。买家可以查看产品资料与收入信号，并在产品确实设为出售时开启私人交易室。

### 为什么创建它

中小型软件产品在收入尚未稳定前，往往很难被发现或出售。传统收购市场通常偏向成熟业务；收入验证服务能够提供证据，却不一定带来曝光；一般付费排名平台能够带来注意力，却缺少进行产品收购所需的背景资料。

BidLadders 的目的，是把这些缺失环节连接起来：产品级证据、容易理解的发现机制、买卖双方的直接对话，以及从 listing 进入第三方 escrow 的实际路径。

### 它解决什么用户问题

对于卖家，BidLadders 解决四个相关问题：

- 即使一个 Stripe 账户内有多个产品，创办人仍可只上架其中一个产品。
- 产品即使是 $0 MRR，或暂时不出售，也可以公开发布。
- 产品资料、收入背景、叫价、营运成本、所含资产及买家对话都保留在同一个 listing 内。
- 曝光规则清楚可见：免费顺序、付费排名及赞助展示不会混为一谈。

对于买家，BidLadders 减少寻找与评估独立产品所需的重复工作。买家可以比较结构化 listing，区分经验证信号与卖家自行提供的资料，确认产品当前是否真正出售，并让谈判始终对应正在评估的具体产品。

### 它如何减少用户压力

BidLadders 通过资料整合、可信保证及明确下一步，减少交易市场中的不确定性。

**资料整合：** 每个 listing 集中展示产品身份、目标用户、解决的问题、技术、定价模式、收入信号、营运成本、叫价、所含资产、卖家身份及当前出售状态。买家不必从零散讯息与截图中自行拼凑基本情况。

**可信保证：** 界面明确区分已知事实与卖家声明。来自 Stripe 产品家族的信号与卖家提供的背景资料分别标示；赞助排名不会被包装成平台背书；listing 代表具体产品，不会默认代表整家 LLC 出售。BidLadders 不代持收购资金，卖家接受交易后才进入第三方 escrow 服务。

**明确下一步：** 卖家可以发布产品、选择是否出售、验证相关 Stripe Product 与 Price、提升曝光并回复买家。买家可以查看 listing、开启私人交易室、商议资产转移，并在卖家接受交易后继续进入 escrow。

### 为什么它独特

BidLadders 把通常分散在不同平台的机制放在同一个产品市场中：

- 为同一 Stripe 账户经营多个产品的创办人提供产品级 listing 与收入归属；
- 允许 $0 MRR 产品，以及只展示而暂不出售的产品合理上架；
- 一个透明的 330 位全局市场，以两个各 165 位的阶梯呈现；
- 免费时间排序、明确标示的付费排名及 24 小时轮播展示同时存在；
- 私人产品交易室连接至有牌照的第三方 escrow；
- 首页稀缺排名之外，仍保留可搜寻的完整产品目录。

因此，BidLadders 既不只是 MRR 验证页面，也不只是付费链接排行榜。它是一个结构化的产品收购市场，让证据、曝光、谈判与成交准备保持连接，同时不把排名冒充为质量证明，也不把 BidLadders 自己包装成资金托管方。
